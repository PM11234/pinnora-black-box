Buyer checklist

How to choose without wasting your trial

Test each product with the same real brief, not just the nicest public demo. Compare your results with five questions:

  1. First useful output: How fast do you get a creative you would actually run?
  2. Brand fidelity: Are colors, product details and messaging accurate?
  3. Variation speed: Can you produce multiple distinct hooks and versions?
  4. True cost: How many credits, revisions and exports does a usable result take?
  5. Repeat workflow: Can you return next week without rebuilding your brand context?

For acquisition, measure cost per activated signup rather than signup volume alone. For retention, monitor repeat creative generation, campaign reuse, paid conversion and D30 activity.

Frequently asked questions

Before choosing your tool

What is the best AI ad generator for ecommerce?

It depends on your workflow. Creatify is worth testing for product-URL video, Arcads for actor-led UGC, Canva Grow for editable ad creation, and Pinnora for broader brand-to-campaign use cases. Test the options with a real product brief.

Can AI-generated ads run on Google and Meta?

AI-generated content can be used in ads, but the final creative, claims, product, landing page and advertiser must comply with each advertising platform's policies. AI-generated people must not be misrepresented as real customer testimonials.

Should I choose based on the cheapest plan?

Not alone. Compare the cost per usable final creative, revision effort, speed, rights and export quality. Confirm current pricing directly with each provider.

Why does this comparison include Pinnora?

This guide is published by Pinnora and includes its own product. We disclose that relationship clearly and avoid treating the list order as an independently verified ranking.
